Yolanda Marie

Yolanda Marie, Yani for short. A traveling interdisciplinary artist, mystical scientist and one ½ of House of Fluid Reflections alongside my love, Shaah. My current focuses are aerial arts, dance, exploring childlike curiosity on canvas, documenting my practical applications of and experiments with quantum theory and manifestation, releasing my first book and music arts. And as always, honoring whatever artistic rabbit holes that summon me.

Personally I'm currently learning Brazilian Portuguese and Spanish, reading What the Bleep Do We Know by William Arntz, Betsy Chasse, and Mark Vicente, delighting in quality family and friends time, especially out in nature, educating myself on personal and business finance and enjoying seasonal fresh fruit.

Ta ta for now.

Peace, Love and Fairydust,
Yolanda Marie | Yani

Artist Statement

My work comes from the body, inspired by the art of movement and the infinite capacity of human form and imagination. I'm not asking you to understand. I'm asking you to feel, the way you feel the energy of a performance hall shift when the stage lights come on.

I call myself both artist and mystical scientist because I trust both instinct and inquiry. Each piece begins as an experiment: with color, with form, with what wants to move through me. What emerges is less a statement and more a frequency. You either feel it, or you don't, I appreciate both responses.

Welcome. I hope something here finds you.
