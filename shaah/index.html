Shaah / Dragon's Pitchfork

Shaah is a Colorado-born, Maryland-raised interdisciplinary artist and Creative Architect & Producer whose work spans music, film, photography, painting, and writing. He approaches each medium as a distinct channel for storytelling and healing, both personal and communal.

A lifelong member of DC-based Wu-Tang Killa Beez collective Tha Beggas (aka The Beggaz / The Beggarz), Shaah has spent the years since expanding his creative reach while actively stewarding the group's legacy through content, coordination, and releases. In 2021, he founded GRYSKLL, a multidisciplinary collective that sits at the center of his current creative focus. He also serves as executive producer on the Killa Bee Movie and co-anchors House of Fluid Reflections alongside his wife, Yolanda Marie.

His visual art has been exhibited at Artomatic DC, Gallery Y, Peace in Movement, and Thundershark Gallery.

gryMatter is where Shaah documents the journey behind all of it, sharing thoughts, resources, and perspective as he builds. It's also where supporters can follow along or contribute to his work.
